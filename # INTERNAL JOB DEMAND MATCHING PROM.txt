
# INTERNAL DEMAND EMAIL GENERATION PROMPT

## PURPOSE

Create an internal email for the selected job demand.

The demand has already been evaluated and scored.

Do not calculate a new score.

Do not re-rank the demands.

Use the demand selected by the previous analysis.

Follow the required company email format.

Follow all information-security rules.

---

## INPUT

Use the following information from the previous analysis:

- Employee Name
- Emp Code
- Employee Grade
- Employee Location
- Skill Breakup
- Selected Demand
- Match Score
- Match Level
- Skill Gap

Use the selected demand data exactly as provided.

---

## INFORMATION SECURITY

The demand information is company-confidential.

The email is for an authorized internal recipient only.

Do not:

- Share the demand sheet.
- Attach the demand sheet.
- Include the complete demand sheet.
- Include unrelated demands.
- Include screenshots.
- Include personal email addresses.
- Include external recipients.
- Mention WhatsApp.
- Mention social media.
- Mention personal devices.
- Mention personal cloud storage.
- Suggest forwarding the demand information externally.
- Add any information that is not required for the internal opportunity.

Use only the information required for the internal job opportunity.

---

## SUBJECT

Use this exact subject format:

Name (Skill)_demand sheet

Example:

Mohan Raj (React)_demand sheet

Rules:

- Use the employee name.
- Use the primary skill from the selected demand.
- Do not add extra words.
- Do not add the match score to the subject.
- Do not add the Indent Id to the subject.

---

## RESOURCE DETAILS

Use this exact format:

| Emp Code | Emp Name | Grade | Location | Skill Breakup |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Emp Code] | [Emp Name] | [Grade] | [Location] | [Relevant skills] |

Use the employee information from the input.

Do not invent missing information.

If a required employee field is not available, use:

[REQUIRED INFORMATION]

Do not guess.

For Skill Breakup, include only skills relevant to the selected demand.

---

## DEMAND DETAILS

Use this exact format:

| Indent Id | Business Relation | Business Unit | Grade | Skill1 | Requester Name | Skill2 | Location | Experience Required | OU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Copy the values from the selected demand.

Do not modify the values.

Do not invent missing values.

Do not add fields that are not present.

---

## EMAIL BODY

Write a concise and professional internal email.

The email must:

1. Address the authorized internal recipient professionally.
2. State that I am interested in the selected internal opportunity.
3. Provide the resource details.
4. Provide the selected demand details.
5. Mention the relevant skill alignment.
6. Mention the match score only if useful.
7. Mention the main skill gap only if useful.
8. Request consideration for the opportunity.
9. End with a professional closing.

Do not include the complete scoring analysis.

Do not include the ranking of other demands.

Do not include unrelated opportunities.

Do not explain the scoring methodology.

Do not include information-security instructions in the email.

---

## WRITING STYLE

Use:

- Professional business English.
- Short sentences.
- Clear wording.
- Direct language.
- Internal corporate tone.

Avoid:

- Excessive explanation.
- Marketing language.
- Unnecessary technical details.
- Claims that are not supported by the input.
- Informal language.
- Emojis.

---

Before producing the email, verify:

[ ] The selected demand comes from the previous analysis.

[ ] No new score is calculated.

[ ] The employee name is correct.

[ ] The Emp Code is not invented.

[ ] The Grade is correct.

[ ] The Location is correct.

[ ] The Skill Breakup is relevant.

[ ] The Indent Id is correct.

[ ] Business Relation is correct.

[ ] Business Unit is correct.

[ ] Demand Grade is correct.

[ ] Skill1 is correct.

[ ] Requester Name is correct.

[ ] Skill2 is correct.

[ ] Demand Location is correct.

[ ] Experience Required is correct.

[ ] OU is correct.

[ ] Subject follows exactly:

Name (Skill)_demand sheet

[ ] The complete demand sheet is not included.

[ ] Unrelated demands are not included.

[ ] No external or unauthorized sharing is suggested.

[ ] No personal-device sharing is suggested.

[ ] No WhatsApp or social-media sharing is suggested.

[ ] No information is invented.

[ ] The email is suitable for an approved company channel.

---

## OUTPUT

Return only the email draft.

Do not return:

- Match analysis
- Score calculation
- Ranking
- Explanation
- Recommendations
- Additional commentary

The output must be ready to send through an approved company email channel.
